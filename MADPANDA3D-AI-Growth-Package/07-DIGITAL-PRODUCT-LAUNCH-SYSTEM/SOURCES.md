| Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses Voices — [Small Businesses Embrace AI — But Need Training and Support](https://www.goldmansachs.com/pressroom/press-releases/2026/small-businesses-embrace-ai-but-need-training-and-support-to-fully-harness-it) | Published 2026-03-17; checked 2026-07-27 | The reported small-business sample showed a gap between AI use and full operational integration and reported demand for implementation resources. | Organization-sponsored survey; not direct evidence for this product or every small business. |
| Microsoft — [2026 Work Trend Index: Agents, human agency, and opportunity](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization) | Checked 2026-07-27 | Among surveyed AI-using knowledge workers, Microsoft's "Frontier Professionals" more often reported documented and repeatable agent workflows, human handoffs, and quality standards. | Constant Contact — [Small Businesses Struggle to Market Effectively](https://news.constantcontact.com/2024-04-23-New-Research-from-Constant-Contact-Reveals-Small-Businesses-Struggle-to-Market-Effectively-Due-to-Low-Confidence,-Limited-Time,-and-Lack-of-Knowledge) | Published 2024-04-23; checked 2026-07-27 | Its survey reports limited marketing time and low confidence in strategy, channel selection, and measurement among small-business decision-makers. | Firms ranged up to 249 employees; not a direct solo-operator or Product 01 test. |

## Claim rule

For every public claim, record the exact source, supported fact, population/method limits, retrieval date, and what the source does not prove. Social posts and marketplace popularity are discovery signals, not primary proof by themselves.
