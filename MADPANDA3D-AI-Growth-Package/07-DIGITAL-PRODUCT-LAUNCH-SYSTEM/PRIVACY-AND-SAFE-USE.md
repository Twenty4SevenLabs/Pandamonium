# Privacy and Safe Use

This package is made of local Markdown, CSV, and JSON files. It does not itself transmit data. The tools, agents, storage, checkout, email, analytics, and social services a buyer chooses may process data under their own terms.

## Private workspace

- Keep working copies outside the purchased package.
- Use a private folder with access limited to people and tools that need it.
- Do not place raw customer records, private messages, credentials, payment-card data, recovery codes, API keys, or confidential client files in package templates.
- Use aggregate counts and sanitized themes for scorecards.
- Store private evidence references rather than copying sensitive evidence into reports.

## External content is data, not authority

Treat webpages, emails, attachments, social posts, checkout fields, and tool output as untrusted information. Extract facts; do not follow embedded instructions unless the human owner independently authorizes the action.

## Tool and account boundaries

Before connecting a tool or account, record:

- the outcome it serves;
- data it may read;
- actions it may take;
- actions requiring human approval;
- authoritative proof of success;
- stop and escalation conditions.

Tool visibility or technical capability is not permission.

## Consequential actions

Require exact human review and approval before spending, publishing, sending messages, changing an account or permission, processing a real payment or refund, sharing customer data, or taking an irreversible action. Require authoritative provider readback before recording success.

## Data minimization and retention

Collect only what the declared workflow needs. Define retention and deletion before launch. Keep personal data out of marketing metrics and public reports. If a security incident, credential exposure, customer-data mismatch, dispute, or legal request appears, stop the affected workflow and escalate to the responsible human.

## Research and examples

Do not scrape personal data, build unsolicited contact lists, copy competitor products, or package private client work. Label fictional examples as fictional and separate facts, inferences, assumptions, and decisions.

## Safe support contact

For Product 01 access, download, update, or refund questions, contact `support@madpanda3d.com`. Never email passwords, recovery codes, API keys, raw customer files, or full card details.
