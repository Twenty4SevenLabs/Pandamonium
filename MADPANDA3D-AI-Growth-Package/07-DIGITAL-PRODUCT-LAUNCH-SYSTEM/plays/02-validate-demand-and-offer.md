# Play 2 — Validate Demand and Offer

## Objective

Test whether credible evidence supports the buyer problem, then define a modest offer and a low-risk validation plan.

## Required inputs

- Passed `BUYER-PROBLEM-BRIEF.md`
- Research access or a manual research process
- A private working copy of `assets/research-matrix.csv`

## Tool capability needed

- **Needed:** web research or manual browsing, source capture, document creation
- **Helpful:** aggregate trend or storefront analytics
- **Manual fallback:** the human opens primary sources and records them in the research matrix

## Ordered actions

1. Turn the hypothesis into research questions about problem frequency, consequence, existing alternatives, willingness to act, and buyer language.
2. Collect current primary sources where possible: official statistics, platform documentation, company reports with disclosed methods, standards, or original surveys.
3. Use marketplaces, forums, search suggestions, and social content only as discovery or language evidence; do not treat popularity alone as proof.
4. Delete the `PLACEHOLDER-DELETE-ME` row. Record every source in the copied `research-matrix.csv`, including retrieval date, exact fact, evidence type, limitations, safe implication, and one classification: `EVIDENCE`, `CONTRARY`, `BACKGROUND`, or `DUPLICATE`.
5. Separate facts from inferences and assumptions. Remove any claim that cannot be traced.
6. Map alternatives by format, outcome, effort, price range when public, and obvious gap. Do not copy wording, structure, brand, or assets.
7. Draft the offer: buyer, problem, deliverable, guided mechanism, outcome, exclusions, price hypothesis, and reason to trust.
8. Draft objections and honest responses. Include “not a fit” conditions.
9. Choose a validation test: contextual interviews, waitlist/interest page, useful public excerpts, or a transparent preorder only if delivery timing and refund handling are ready and approved.
10. Define pass, revise, and stop thresholds before running the test.
11. Save `work/02-offer/OFFER-EVIDENCE-PACKET.md` and update `STATUS.md`.

## Produced artifact

`OFFER-EVIDENCE-PACKET.md` containing:

- dated evidence table and source URLs;
- limitations and contradictions;
- alternative map;
- buyer language notes;
- offer statement and claim boundaries;
- objections and fit filters;
- validation test with thresholds;
- unsupported claims to avoid.

## Success criteria

- At least three credible sources are recorded, including at least one current primary source when one is reasonably available.
- Every material claim maps to a source or direct demonstration.
- Facts, inferences, assumptions, and decisions are visibly separated.
- The offer promises a process and deliverable the owner can produce, not guaranteed revenue.
- The validation test has a defined audience, timebox, metric, and pass/revise/stop threshold.
- No personal data scraping, cold mass outreach, fabricated scarcity, or copied competitor material is proposed.

## Stop conditions

- Evidence materially contradicts the problem or desired outcome.
- Only low-quality secondary claims can be found and direct validation is impossible.
- The offer requires an unsupported performance, compliance, or income claim.
- The owner will not define a test threshold before seeing results.

## Next play

Proceed to Play 3 only with a bounded, evidence-supported offer. Otherwise revise Play 1 or stop.
