# Play 7 — Build the Organic Launch

## Objective

Create a useful, non-spam organic campaign that demonstrates the product's method, offers real value, and measures qualified interest.

## Required inputs

- QA-passed package
- Independently QA-passed buyer-path plan and exact package hash
- Approved channels/accounts and their current connection state
- A private working copy of `assets/content-calendar.csv`
- Posting/engagement limits and owner approval rules

## Tool capability needed

- **Needed:** document creation
- **Helpful:** approved social publishing/scheduling and aggregate analytics
- **Manual fallback:** create local drafts and a manual posting checklist; do not claim they were published

## Ordered actions

1. Confirm exact approved channels and account identities. A connected account is not automatically approved; an expired account is not ready.
2. Define three content pillars: show one play producing one artifact, give one useful play/excerpt away, and show honest proof or revision.
3. Create platform-specific briefs rather than pasting identical copy everywhere.
4. For each draft, define buyer, problem, useful takeaway, evidence/demo, honest CTA, destination, source tag, and claim guard.
5. Use the calendar to keep cadence sustainable. Avoid mass posting, copied comments, cold messages, fake engagement, or more than the owner's approved limit.
6. Define contextual engagement: reply only to real inbound or relevant public context, personalize the response, add specific value, and send no more than one unanswered follow-up.
7. Preflight every external action: exact account, content, link/tag, timing, cost, approval, and required provider readback.
8. Before any launch activation or publication, open `plays/08-run-the-30-day-loop.md` and complete only its prelaunch rule-design action. Save and date/freeze `work/08-scorecard/PRELAUNCH-DECISION-RULES.md`; do not start the measurement interval yet.
9. Save and date/freeze `work/07-launch/ORGANIC-LAUNCH-PLAN.md`, verify every Play 7 success criterion plus the exact prelaunch-rule artifact, checkpoint `STATUS.md`, and mark the Play 7 gate `PASS` or remain blocked.
10. Only after action 9 passes may the owner approve an exact checkout-activation, publication, or scheduling action. Execute only through the reviewed process/tool with authoritative readback; record success after readback, not after drafting.
11. Collect aggregate views/visits, qualified visits, checkout handoffs, orders, and substantive questions without storing private message bodies in analytics.

## Produced artifact

`work/07-launch/ORGANIC-LAUNCH-PLAN.md` containing:

- approved channel/account matrix;
- content pillars and 30-day cadence;
- platform-specific content briefs and local drafts;
- contextual engagement policy;
- first-party campaign-tag taxonomy;
- approval and readback checklist;
- measurement definitions;
- dated/frozen `work/08-scorecard/PRELAUNCH-DECISION-RULES.md` created before activation;
- capability/access blockers.

## Success criteria

- Every planned channel and account is explicitly approved and currently usable, or clearly marked draft-only.
- Each post teaches, demonstrates, or reports evidence; none depends on manufactured urgency or unsupported outcomes.
- Drafts are meaningfully adapted by platform.
- Cadence respects the owner's per-platform rolling-window limit.
- Source tags are first-party and allowlisted.
- Publication, messaging, and account changes require explicit approval and authoritative readback.
- The plan can run at the approved budget, including zero.
- Prelaunch decision rules are dated and frozen before activation or publication; the interval has not been started early.
- The exact launch plan and rules are saved, verified, checkpointed, and `PASS` before activation, publication, or scheduling can be approved.

## Stop conditions

- Exact account approval is missing.
- The connector lacks the requested post/reply/comment/direct-message action.
- A draft advertises a feature not in the released archive.
- The plan relies on scraped personal data, cold mass outreach, spam, fake reviews, or paid spend beyond budget.

If the declared start is a qualified-visit event, the zero-order path may begin before a paid order; if no valid start event occurs, keep the interval `NOT_STARTED`. Educational prelaunch drafts still require approval and honest availability labels.
