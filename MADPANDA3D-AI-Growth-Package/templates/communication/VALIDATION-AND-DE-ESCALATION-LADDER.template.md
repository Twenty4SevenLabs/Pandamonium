## Human and Resource Routes

Create separate decisions for the required routine or human-review route,
`CRISIS RESOURCE`, and `IMMEDIATE DANGER ROUTE`. An untriggered route is `NOT
REQUIRED` with a reason and owner, not evidence that the person is safe.

## Reviews and Readiness

- Declared evaluation order:
- First failed or unknown required row:
- Dependent rows marked `NOT RUN`:
- Independent supplied evidence preserved:
- First next-owner task:
- Deferred blockers and reasons:
- Truth-and-role review owner:
- Truth-and-role review: `PENDING` / `PASS` / `REPAIR`
- Support-and-escalation review owner:
- Support-and-escalation review: `PENDING` / `PASS` / `REPAIR`
- `READY FOR COM-08: YES` / `READY FOR COM-08: NO`

`READY FOR COM-08: YES` requires every required `EXP-##` sourced and
classified; every required `LDR-##` evidence-linked, truthful, role-honest,
privacy-minimized, channel-fit, and authorized; every required `ESC-##` current,
locale-aware, owned, and `PASS`; justified `NOT REQUIRED` routes; no unsupported
emotion, diagnosis, agreement, safety, promise, confidentiality, or relationship
claim; and both reviews `PASS`. Any consequential `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT
RUN`, stale resource, missing human owner, unsupported authority, or required
review at `REPAIR` makes readiness `NO`. This artifact authorizes no send,
contact, disclosure, counseling, emergency call, intervention, or other action.
