`COMMUNICATION CAPSTONE VERIFIED: YES` requires a complete shared contract;
one evidence-supported behavior gap; one selected smallest supported adaptation
layer; all required candidate and selection rows `PASS`; supplied paired tests
with frozen cases, held controls, one declared variable, independent scoring,
task outcome, critical, privacy, rights, and adverse-effect gates complete; a
bounded disposition, rollback, expiry, unresolved-risk, and follow-up record;
no invented case, response, score, effect, approval, implementation, deployment,
fine-tuning, success, or general-quality claim; and both reviews `PASS`. A
`TRAINING-DATA-MODEL` candidate also requires explicit supplied data, rights,
privacy, safety, security, risk, evaluation, rollback, cost, and authority
decisions or it remains deferred. A
proposed or untested candidate remains `NOT RUN`. A local blocker stops only its
dependents, preserves independent supplied evidence, makes both reviews
`REPAIR`, and makes the capstone result `NO`. This artifact authorizes no prompt,
retrieval, tool, model, data, record, channel, workflow, deployment, purchase,
send, contact, or training change.
