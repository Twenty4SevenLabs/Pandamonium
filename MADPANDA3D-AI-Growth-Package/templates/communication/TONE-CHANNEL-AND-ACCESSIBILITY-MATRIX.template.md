## Reviews and Readiness

- Blockers:
- Smallest owner-assigned task:
- Meaning-preservation review: `PENDING` / `PASS` / `REPAIR`
- Channel-access review: `PENDING` / `PASS` / `REPAIR`
- `READY FOR COM-06: YES` / `READY FOR COM-06: NO`

Use only stated or consented preferences. Do not infer intent, culture,
attention, emotion, or disability from channel cues or their absence. Do not
depend on one sensory or presentation characteristic. `READY FOR COM-06: YES`
requires every protected segment mapped across primary and fallback channels;
every required channel, alternative, and loss-test row at `PASS`; sourced
preferences; current channel evidence; and both reviews at `PASS`. Any `REPAIR`,
`UNKNOWN`, failed test, or meaning change makes readiness `NO`. This artifact
does not authorize delivery, interface changes, or a WCAG conformance claim.
