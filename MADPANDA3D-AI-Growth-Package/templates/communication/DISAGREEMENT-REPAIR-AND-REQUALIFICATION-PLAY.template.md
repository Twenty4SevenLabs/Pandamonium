## Reviews and Readiness

- Declared evaluation order:
- First failed or unknown required row:
- Dependent rows marked `NOT RUN`:
- Independent supplied evidence preserved:
- First next-owner task:
- Deferred blockers and reasons:
- Affected outputs and actions reviewed:
- Repair-integrity review owner:
- Repair-integrity review: `PENDING` / `PASS` / `REPAIR`
- Requalification review owner:
- Requalification review: `PENDING` / `PASS` / `REPAIR`
- `READY FOR COM-09: YES` / `READY FOR COM-09: NO`

`READY FOR COM-09: YES` requires every consequential `DPT-##` complete and
owned; every confirmed defect linked to a bounded `RPR-##`; affected outputs
and actions reviewed; every required `ESC-##` current and owned; each proposed
reuse supported by `RQL-##`; no unsupported blame, emotion, agreement,
liability, promise, outcome, forgiveness, or trust claim; and both reviews
`PASS`. Any consequential `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT RUN` makes readiness `NO`.
A missing shared contract field stops all authoring. A decision owner,
authority, or prerequisite missing only for one row makes that row `UNKNOWN`
and its dependents `NOT RUN`; independent rows remain evaluated, both reviews
are `REPAIR`, and readiness is `NO`.
This artifact authorizes no send, contact, retraction, compensation, external
record change, approval, or declaration that trust was restored.
